You are evaluating one text chunk as possible explanatory evidence for a
known target category of a computer-science research paper.

The target category is supplied because this is an offline teacher-supervision
task. Do not predict, verify, or replace the target category. Your task is to
judge how well the chunk explains substantive membership in that target.

Evaluate only the supplied chunk. Do not assume access to the paper's title,
abstract, filename, source path, retrieval rank, vector distance, surrounding
chunks, or other metadata.

TARGET-CONDITIONED EVALUATION

Before assigning scores, apply this checklist internally:

1. Identify the technical object discussed in the chunk.
2. Identify what the chunk says about that object: a problem, method,
   analysis, result, or contribution.
3. Compare that object and contribution with the supplied target description.
4. Decide whether the connection is substantive, incidental, or absent.
5. Separate substantive evidence from direct category disclosure and isolated
   keywords.

Do not compare the target with other categories. Do not output a predicted
category. Apply the checklist internally and output only the required JSON
object.

TARGET EVIDENCE

target_evidence measures how strongly the chunk could support a faithful,
technical explanation that the paper belongs to the supplied target category.

0 = No meaningful support. The chunk is unrelated, uninterpretable, purely
    administrative, or contains only a category name without substantive
    target-related content.

1 = Weak support. The connection is incidental, generic, or based mainly on
    isolated terms; the target-related research object or contribution is not
    established.

2 = Plausible support. A target-related object, problem, method, result, or
    contribution is visible, but the connection is incomplete, secondary, or
    substantially ambiguous.

3 = Clear support. The chunk clearly connects a target-related research object
    to a concrete problem, method, analysis, result, or contribution.

4 = Strong and detailed support. The chunk gives multiple connected technical
    details that would make a precise, faithful explanation of target-category
    membership possible.

Target evidence does not require the category name to appear. A chunk that
only states or repeats the target label should receive target_evidence 0 or 1,
depending on whether it contains any additional substantive support.

EXPLANATORY QUALITY

substantive_relevance

0 = No useful research content.
1 = Generic, malformed, administrative, or tangential content.
2 = Meaningful research content with limited explanatory value.
3 = Clear information about a research problem, method, result, or
    contribution.
4 = Rich evidence that could strongly support an explanation.

specificity

0 = Entirely generic.
1 = Mostly vague or broad.
2 = Contains some concrete technical information.
3 = Clearly describes a technical object, problem, method, result, or
    contribution.
4 = Connects multiple concrete technical elements precisely.

context_completeness

0 = Uninterpretable.
1 = Severe missing context.
2 = Partly understandable but substantially incomplete.
3 = Mostly understandable and usable on its own.
4 = Self-contained and clearly interpretable.

SHORTCUT RISKS

direct_label_disclosure

0 = No target-category disclosure.
1 = A broad field name appears without claiming category membership.
2 = The work is broadly positioned within the target field.
3 = The chunk explicitly states that the work belongs to the target category.
4 = The chunk directly exposes an arXiv category, category code, dataset label,
    or equivalent classification metadata.

keyword_only_risk

0 = Target support is based on meaningful technical relationships, or there
    is no apparent target cue.
1 = Target support is mostly contextual with little keyword dependence.
2 = Target support mixes contextual content with broad target-related terms.
3 = Apparent target support relies mainly on isolated target-related words.
4 = Apparent target support consists almost entirely of a category label or
    isolated keywords.

SCORING CONSISTENCY

Keep the dimensions separate:

- A chunk may be technically rich but provide little evidence for this target.
- A chunk may expose the target label but contain little substantive evidence.
- A chunk may strongly support the target without naming it directly.
- A method borrowed from the target field is weak target evidence when the
  chunk studies a different object and gives no target-specific contribution.
- High direct_label_disclosure or keyword_only_risk must not increase
  target_evidence.
- The brief reason must agree with all numeric scores.

The brief_reason must contain one concise English sentence of no more than
40 words. State the strongest target-specific evidence and the main
limitation. Do not output a long analysis or hidden reasoning.
